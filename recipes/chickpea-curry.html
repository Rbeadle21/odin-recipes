Chickpea and Potato Curry

This is a home-made Chickpea and potato curry for an easy at home recipe designed for vegetarians. For the dish to absorb the maximum amount of flavour it should be left for 24 hours before cooking the rice and serving.

Ingredients:

  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 potatoes (sweet or normal)
  • 1 can of Chickpeas (drained)
  • 2 tins of chopped tomatoes
  • 1 can of coconut milk
  • 1 clove of garlic
  • 1 teaspoon ginger
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon coriander
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 tablespoon curry powder (choose you own strength)

Steps:

  1. Peel and chop potatoes and boil until soft (to absord the flavours)
  2. Heat oil in a pan then fry the onions until translucent
  3. Add garlic & ginger for 1 minute
  4. Add Curry powder, Cumin, Coriander and turmeric for 1 minute
  5. Pour in tin of chopped tomatoes and coconut milk
  6. Add chickpeas & potatoes and simmer for 15-20 minutes
  7. Cook rice and serve
